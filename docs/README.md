# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project management process summary
OctoAcme follows a lightweight, iterative project management lifecycle for cross-functional delivery:

1. **Initiation** — validate the problem and business outcome, identify stakeholders, define success criteria and milestones, and make a go/no-go decision for planning.
2. **Planning** — turn approved initiatives into prioritized, estimable backlog items with acceptance criteria, dependencies, risks, and a release/milestone plan.
3. **Execution and tracking** — deliver work through project board flow, pull requests, CI checks, regular standups/syncs, demos, metrics, and blocker escalation.
4. **Risk management and communication** — maintain a risk register, monitor impact/likelihood, communicate regular status updates, and escalate through defined paths.
5. **Release and deployment** — confirm acceptance criteria and CI/security checks, prepare release notes and rollback plans, deploy with smoke tests, and validate post-deploy health.
6. **Retrospective and continuous improvement** — review what went well and what to improve, assign actionable follow-ups, and track impact over time.
7. **Roles and personas** — clarify how developers, product managers, project managers, and stakeholders collaborate and own outcomes.
